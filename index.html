Our Heritage

Crafting Excellence Since 1987

For over three decades, Aurelia has been synonymous with exceptional craftsmanship and timeless design. Founded in the heart of Paris, our atelier brings together master artisans who have dedicated their lives to the art of fine jewelry.

Each piece in our collection tells a story — of meticulous attention to detail, of ethically sourced gemstones, and of the passion that drives us to create jewelry that transcends generations. We believe that true luxury lies not in ostentation, but in the quiet confidence of exquisite craftsmanship.

From the initial sketch to the final polish, every Aurelia piece undergoes over 200 hours of handcrafting, ensuring that what you wear is not merely jewelry, but a work of art.
